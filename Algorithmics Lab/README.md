## 🧩 Algorithmics Lab

This game is my first ever university project (note the quality 🙃), a terminal-based dungeon crawler built with ncurses, where you play as a former cook seeking revenge against the villainous Chef Passadini. You explore procedurally generated maps, move in eight directions, fight enemies with directional or area attacks, avoid traps, and collect items that improve your stats. As you progress through doors, you climb floors and face increasing difficulty, with stronger enemy types appearing over time.

Your character grows by picking up upgrades for vision, attack range, damage, healing, and maximum health, while managing survival in tighter and more dangerous encounters. The goal is to clear all 15 levels and reach the final floor, where Passadini appears as the boss. Defeating him wins the game and completes the revenge-driven journey.

🎯 Skills acquired: imperative programming, data structures
